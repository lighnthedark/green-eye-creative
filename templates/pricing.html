The two ways to buy

Per relationship, not per word. AYRT charges the instructor or institution once — every student session is covered. Stickier contracts, higher lifetime value than competitors' per-word meters.

Every plan starts with a pilot. AYRT: a free term in one classroom — see the real-time certification in action. TTC: a free 30-day pilot on Professional, run against your real pipeline — see what it catches before you pay.

TTC screens submissions after the fact and flags signals for human review. AYRT certifies authorship in real time, as the work is written — not a screen, a live record. Neither issues automated verdicts; both leave the decision with you. Patent pending across the portfolio.
